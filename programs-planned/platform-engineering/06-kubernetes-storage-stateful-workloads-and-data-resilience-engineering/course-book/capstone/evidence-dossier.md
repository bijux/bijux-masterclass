# Atlas data-plane evidence dossier

The final dossier is the release argument for Atlas Data Plane 1.0. It is not a folder of screenshots. Every artifact answers a decision question and records its evidence class.

## Required contents

1. exact Course 5 predecessor manifest;
2. data catalogue and responsibility matrix;
3. StorageClass and mutable-attribute contracts;
4. driver and sidecar capability matrix;
5. topology and capacity analysis;
6. PVC/PV binding transcript;
7. CSI lifecycle and ambiguous-retry evidence;
8. StatefulSet identity and fencing proof;
9. snapshot/group-snapshot consistency record;
10. backup manifest and empty-destination restore report;
11. RPO and RTO calculation with executed phase times;
12. performance, quota, and growth report;
13. migration ledger and rollback boundary;
14. planted-defect results;
15. categorical release decision;
16. exact Course 7 handoff.

## Evidence fields

Each record includes subject identity, environment, tool or component version, command or experiment, expected oracle, observed result, timestamp, residual risk, and the strongest justified claim. Missing native or operated evidence is written as `not-executed`, never implied by a semantic fixture.
