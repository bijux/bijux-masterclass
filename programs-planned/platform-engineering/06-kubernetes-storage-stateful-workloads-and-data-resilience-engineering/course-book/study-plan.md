# Ten-day study plan

Each numbered module is one dense teaching day. A realistic day contains three modes: read and annotate the chapter, solve the transfer exercises without consulting the answers, then perform the laboratory and checkpoint. The laboratory is not a demonstration to watch; it is a controlled claim you must attempt to falsify.

| Day | Focus | Atlas result |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | data lifetime and authority | data-classification and ownership map |
| 2 | PV, PVC, StorageClass, and binding | governed storage catalogue and binder |
| 3 | CSI controller and node lifecycle | idempotent provision/attach/mount model |
| 4 | topology, capacity, expansion, and QoS | schedulable storage and mutation policy |
| 5 | StatefulSet identity and fencing | exclusive writer and ordinal recovery model |
| 6 | database, object-store, and operator boundaries | explicit placement and ownership decisions |
| 7 | snapshot, clone, population, and consistency | consistency-aware copy and snapshot model |
| 8 | backup, restore, RPO, RTO, and DR | empty-destination recovery evidence |
| 9 | performance, capacity, and diagnosis | storage SLOs and failure-signature catalogue |
| 10 | migration, maintenance, release, and handoff | Atlas Data Plane 1.0 release dossier |

Do not advance because a manifest applied. Advance when the checkpoint evidence supports the module’s governing claim.
