# Checkpoint — schedulability and resource policy

## Required evidence

- Atlas request/limit and QoS calculation;
- per-Node scheduling explanation;
- topology-domain inventory;
- minimum-safe-repair decision;
- statement of unproved capacity claims.

## Decision defense

Defend why Atlas is Burstable rather than Guaranteed in Course 4 and what evidence would justify changing it.

## Progression gate

Proceed only when scheduling failure can be explained without reference to current utilization alone.
