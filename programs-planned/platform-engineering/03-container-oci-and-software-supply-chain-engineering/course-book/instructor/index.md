# Instructor notes

These pages support facilitated delivery while preserving the course’s self-learning design.

- [Teaching notes](teaching-notes.md) identifies the conceptual pivots that deserve classroom time.
- [Laboratory oracles](lab-oracles.md) explains the evidence expected from each controlled failure.
- [Misconceptions](misconceptions.md) lists plausible but dangerous shortcuts to challenge directly.
- [Accessibility and self-study](accessibility-and-self-study.md) suggests pacing, alternatives to privileged laboratories, and review methods.

The instructor should not turn the course into a product certification class. The learner must be able to transfer the reasoning to another builder, runtime, registry, or signing system.
