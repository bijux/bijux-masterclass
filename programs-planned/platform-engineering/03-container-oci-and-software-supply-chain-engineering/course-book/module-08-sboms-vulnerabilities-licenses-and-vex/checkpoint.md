# Checkpoint — Inventory and vulnerability evidence

## Required evidence

- subject-bound CycloneDX BOM;
- completeness comparison;
- vulnerability decision set;
- one bounded VEX statement;
- stale-VEX rejection;
- license review outcome;
- evidence freshness record.

## Decision defense

Defend one not-affected decision and one blocked finding without relying only on scanner severity.

## Progression gate

Proceed when the release inventory is exact enough to support verification policy. Module 9 will prove who built and signed the subject—and why signatures alone still do not authorize it.
