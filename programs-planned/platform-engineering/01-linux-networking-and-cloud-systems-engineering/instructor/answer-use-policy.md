# Worked-Answer Use Policy

Learners complete all twelve exercises before opening the answers. The answers are models of reasoning, not phrases to memorize.

A different answer is acceptable when it:

- identifies the correct owner and transition;
- preserves the relevant security, durability, or availability boundary;
- names evidence that can distinguish competing explanations;
- states the scope and limitation of the claim;
- avoids relying on a tool default as universal behavior.

During review, ask the learner to change one assumption—filesystem, address family, supervisor, failure domain, or trust source—and adapt the answer. This tests transfer rather than recall.
