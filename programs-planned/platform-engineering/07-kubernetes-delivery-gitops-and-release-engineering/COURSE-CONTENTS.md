# Course contents

**Kubernetes Delivery, GitOps, and Release Engineering** is the seventh course in the Platform Engineering program. It consumes Atlas Data Plane 1.0 from Course 6 and produces Atlas Delivery Plane 1.0 plus the security and tenancy handoff for Course 8.

## Learner product

The authoritative course book contains an orientation, ten full modules, 120 exercises with matching worked answers, ten failure-oriented laboratories, ten evidence checkpoints, reader guides, technical reference maps, an incident casebook, a capstone manual, and the final defense.

## Executable capstone

The capstone includes:

- a deterministic Python delivery model;
- an independent Go digest and drift probe;
- Kustomize bases and dev, staging, and production overlays;
- a versioned Helm chart with values schema;
- Argo CD AppProject, Applications, ApplicationSet, hooks, waves, and prune policy;
- Flux source, Kustomization, HelmRelease, image automation, and notification resources;
- SOPS and External Secrets teaching lanes;
- Argo Rollouts and Flagger progressive-delivery resources;
- protected data-plane inventory;
- fleet catalogues and promotion waves;
- release candidates and ten planted defects;
- exact Course 6 predecessor and Course 8 successor handoffs.

## Learning outcome

A graduate can reconstruct a release from immutable artifacts to desired state, distinguish rendering from reconciliation and health, design controller authority, protect data from unsafe prune, manage secret adoption, evaluate progressive exposure, recover from source or controller failure, and publish a categorical evidence dossier.
