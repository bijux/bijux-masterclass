# Ten-day study plan

The course is designed as ten demanding engineering days after orientation. A slower self-study pace is acceptable; preserve module order because each day adds authority to the same release system.

| Day | Reading and practical result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | [Delivery authority and reconciliation](module-01-delivery-systems-gitops-authority-and-reconciliation/index.md): create the release state and authority map. |
| 2 | [KRM, Kustomize, and render integrity](module-02-kubernetes-resource-model-kustomize-and-render-integrity/index.md): produce deterministic environment renders and canonical digests. |
| 3 | [Helm chart engineering](module-03-helm-chart-engineering-packaging-and-upgrade-semantics/index.md): define and test the chart input and upgrade API. |
| 4 | [Repository topology and promotion](module-04-repository-topology-artifact-promotion-and-environment-lineage/index.md): promote immutable subjects with predecessor lineage. |
| 5 | [Argo CD](module-05-argo-cd-application-reconciliation-health-and-prune/index.md): design Application, Project, inventory, health, and protected prune. |
| 6 | [Flux](module-06-flux-controller-composition-sources-and-image-automation/index.md): trace source artifacts, controller dependencies, and candidate automation. |
| 7 | [Secrets and artifact trust](module-07-secrets-credentials-artifact-trust-and-policy-gates/index.md): prove source, materialization, adoption, and revocation. |
| 8 | [Progressive delivery](module-08-progressive-delivery-analysis-rollback-and-data-compatibility/index.md): execute a falsifiable canary and safe abort. |
| 9 | [Fleet and recovery](module-09-multi-cluster-fleet-bootstrap-controller-lifecycle-and-recovery/index.md): promote through topology-aware waves and recover the control plane. |
| 10 | [Release operations and final defense](module-10-release-operations-incidents-final-defense-and-security-handoff/index.md): publish the dossier and defend the Course 8 handoff. |

Budget approximately two hours for the chapter and exercises, three hours for implementation and lab work, and one hour for evidence review and checkpoint. Stop when a gate fails. Repairing the evidence chain is the lesson; rushing to the next module defeats the cumulative design.
