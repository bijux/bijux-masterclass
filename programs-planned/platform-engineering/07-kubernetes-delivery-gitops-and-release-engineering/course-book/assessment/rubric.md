# Assessment rubric

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Desired-state and identity model | 15 |
| Rendering and configuration architecture | 15 |
| Reconciliation, inventory, health, and prune | 20 |
| Secrets, credentials, and artifact trust | 15 |
| Progressive delivery and rollback | 15 |
| Fleet recovery and controller lifecycle | 10 |
| Final evidence dossier and defense | 10 |

Passing requires 85/100. Immutable identity, protected-resource safety, secret non-disclosure, data compatibility, and categorical release honesty are non-compensable.

## Extended reader notes

Score 100 points: delivery model and ownership 15; render and packaging integrity 15; repository and promotion lineage 10; controller reconciliation and inventory 15; secret and artifact trust 10; progressive delivery and data compatibility 15; fleet and recovery 10; evidence dossier and communication 10. A score of 85 is required.

The following are non-compensable: wrong artifact subject, data-owner authority violation, unsafe prune, rollback claim beyond the frontier, and missing blocker evidence represented as pass. Reviewers record both score and these categorical gates.
