# Final defense

The learner receives five compound failures: moved artifact tag, hidden render change, protected prune, misleading canary, and repository outage during controller recovery. The defense must identify the earliest failed transition, contain authority, preserve data, produce a categorical decision, reconcile emergency state back into Git, and verify the Course 8 handoff.

A tool demonstration without ownership and evidence reasoning does not pass.

## Extended reader notes

The defense has three rounds. In the first, present the normal release from Course 6 handoff to production dossier, showing immutable identities and every authority transition. In the second, diagnose one compound failure selected by the reviewer. In the third, defend the Course 8 security handoff and identify claims that remain environment-backed.

Non-compensable failures include mutable production artifacts, unauthorized protected deletion, evidence about the wrong subject, missing data compatibility, silent unavailable metrics, and inability to restore reviewed desired state after break glass. Tool fluency cannot compensate for these failures.

The reviewer may ask the learner to switch from the primary controller to the comparative controller vocabulary. The engineering answer should remain stable even when object names change.
