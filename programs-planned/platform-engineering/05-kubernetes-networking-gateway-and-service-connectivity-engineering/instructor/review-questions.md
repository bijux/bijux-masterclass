# Instructor review questions

Use these questions throughout the course:

- What is the complete forward and return packet path?
- Which API object expresses intent, and which process or data plane implements it?
- Which transition failed first?
- What state can remain healthy while that transition fails?
- Which address family, node, zone, namespace, and protocol were tested?
- Is the observed identity a transport address, workload identity, service identity, or authenticated user identity?
- What negative control proves the observation is sensitive to the mechanism under review?
- Did the repair change policy, TLS, retry, readiness, or workload authority?
- Is the behavior normative, implementation-specific, Atlas-profiled, or merely observed in one environment?
- What evidence remains cluster-, cloud-, or operations-backed?
- What exactly must Course 6 inherit, and what must it prove independently?
