# Checkpoint — Locality and dual stack

## Required evidence

- locality policy decision
- source-address map by path
- forced IPv4 and IPv6 proof plan
- zone-loss outcome populations
- session-affinity risk analysis
- completed locality lab

## Decision defense

Defend whether locality is a preference or requirement and quantify the availability consequence of no local endpoint.

## Progression gate

Progress only when the evidence is reproducible and your explanation separates desired policy, selected endpoints, programmed data plane, and observed protocol behavior.
