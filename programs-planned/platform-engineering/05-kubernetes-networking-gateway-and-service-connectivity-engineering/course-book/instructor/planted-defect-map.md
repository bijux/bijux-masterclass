# Instructor planted-defect map

The ten defects span the course progression:

1. route or MTU confusion;
2. leaked or conflicting IPAM attachment;
3. Service/endpoint port mismatch;
4. stale negative DNS cache;
5. additive-policy misunderstanding;
6. source-identity loss under exposure;
7. unresolved or unauthorized Gateway reference;
8. TLS identity or backend-protocol mismatch;
9. unsafe egress peer or retry amplification;
10. unsupported production claim or incomplete handoff.

Do not grade merely on finding the defect label. Grade the evidence chain that distinguishes the defect from adjacent hypotheses.
