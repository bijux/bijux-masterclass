# Assessment rubric

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| packet-path and ownership reasoning | 15 |
| CNI/IPAM lifecycle correctness | 10 |
| Service, endpoint, and DNS correctness | 15 |
| segmentation and negative controls | 15 |
| Gateway API and TLS trust boundaries | 15 |
| egress and service-connectivity decisions | 10 |
| troubleshooting and evidence quality | 10 |
| release and Course 6 handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

A score of 85 is required. The non-compensable failures in the final-defense page override the numerical total.

Excellent work identifies ownership, shows positive and negative evidence, explains residual uncertainty, and chooses the smallest justified mechanism. Weak work lists commands, restarts components without a hypothesis, or treats one successful request as proof of a platform guarantee.
