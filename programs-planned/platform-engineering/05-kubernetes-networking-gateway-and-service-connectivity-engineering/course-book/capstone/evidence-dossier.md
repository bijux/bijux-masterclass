# Capstone evidence dossier

The final dossier contains:

1. predecessor integrity record;
2. version and implementation inventory;
3. address-domain and packet-path maps;
4. CNI lifecycle and IPAM evidence;
5. Service/EndpointSlice and named-port evidence;
6. DNS positive, negative, A, AAAA, and cache evidence;
7. effective policy matrix with positive and negative controls;
8. external exposure and source-identity record;
9. Gateway status and protocol probes;
10. TLS chain, hostname, key-usage, and rotation evidence;
11. egress destination-to-peer record;
12. direct-versus-mesh decision;
13. planted-defect rejection report;
14. release classification;
15. exact Course 6 handoff manifest;
16. residual-risk and unexecuted-evidence list.

Every artifact should identify its evidence class. A local-cluster success must not be copied into the environment-backed section. The final defense is evaluated on the quality of reasoning and proof sensitivity, not on the number of screenshots.
