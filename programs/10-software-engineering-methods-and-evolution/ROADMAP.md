# 10. Software Engineering: Methods & Evolution

**Program code:** `SEME`

**Purpose:** Develop disciplined methods for discovering, planning, building, maintaining, and changing software across its full economic and organizational lifecycle.

**Level-II professional outcome:** Ready to choose and adapt development methods, run effective Scrum or Kanban systems, manage requirements and risk, and lead the evolution of long-lived software.

**Prerequisites:** software construction experience; basic teamwork and version control; one substantial software project.

**Primary ownership:** software lifecycle models; requirements engineering; agile and lean methods; Scrum and Kanban; planning and estimation; configuration and change management; maintenance and refactoring; legacy modernization; software economics; socio-technical evolution.

**Explicit exclusions:** code-level design (Program 09); architecture ownership (Program 12); testing techniques (Program 13); delivery pipeline mechanics and production reliability (Program 14).
